title: Getting started
description: Sign in, enter your assigned workspace, navigate the application, and adjust the display theme.
---

## Sign in

1. Open the Priv-View login page.
2. Enter the **email address** and **password** supplied by your administrator.
3. Select **Sign in**.

Priv-View resolves your assigned tenant after authentication; tenant identifiers are not login credentials. Your session is kept in protected browser cookies. If the session expires while you are working, Priv-View attempts to refresh it automatically. Use the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar to sign out.

## Navigate the workspace

The primary navigation contains:

- **Overview** for a summary of the selected tenant.
- **Clients** for client workspaces, matters, and evidence collections.
- **Users** for users belonging to the selected tenant.
- **Documentation** for this searchable help center.
- **Tenants**, visible only to a root super administrator, for tenant management.

On smaller screens, use the menu button in the top bar to open navigation. On larger screens, the sidebar can be collapsed.

## Select a tenant

A root super administrator can switch between the root tenant and its subtenants using the tenant selector in the sidebar. Lists and actions then operate in the selected tenant.

Regular tenant users remain in their assigned tenant and do not see the selector.

## Change the theme

Use the theme control in the top-right corner to choose light, dark, or system appearance. The system setting follows the appearance preference of your device.
