---
title: Entities
description: Keep the matter's directory of people and organizations accurate so facts name the right owner and participants.
---

**Entities** are the people and organizations that own or take part in facts. Each matter keeps its own directory. An entity can appear in documents under several names and email addresses (for example "Jane Smith", "J. Smith" and jane.smith@example.com); these are kept as **variants** of one entity, so every fact that names any of them counts toward the same person.

Agents add people and organizations they find in documents as **Proposed**. Reviewers confirm the right ones, reject wrong ones, and merge duplicates.

## Who can use it

| Permission | What it allows |
| --- | --- |
| View entities | Open **Entities** in the matter menu and entity pages. |
| Manage entities | Add, edit, confirm, reject, merge and split entities. |

Matter administrators and reviewers can manage entities. Analysis operators and read-only users can view them. The list of an entity's facts also requires fact access.

## Browse the directory

1. Open the matter and select **Entities** in the matter menu.
2. **Directory** lists confirmed people and organizations. Switch the status menu to **Rejected** to see rejected ones.
3. Search by name or email address (any variant matches), and narrow to people or organizations.

Each row shows the entity's name and other names, type, title and organization, email address, how many facts it is part of, and its status. Select a name to open the entity.

To add someone the agents have not found, choose **New entity**. Entities you add are confirmed immediately.

## Review proposed entities

**Proposed** lists the people and organizations agents found that no one has reviewed yet. Confirm the ones that are right. Reject the ones that are wrong: a rejected entity is removed as owner or participant from every fact that names it (the facts themselves are kept), and you are asked to confirm first. If a proposed entity is someone already in the directory, open it and merge it instead.

## Work with one entity

- **Edit** the name, title, organization, custodian link and description. Only people have a title and an organization.
- **Confirm** or **Reject** it. A rejected entity can be confirmed again later.
- **Merge into…** combines duplicates: search for the entity to keep and choose **Keep this one**. Its names, email addresses and facts move to that entity.
- **Names and email addresses** lists every variant. Add a name or address the entity also uses, or choose **Split off** to separate a variant into its own entity (this undoes a merge for that name or address).
- **Facts** lists the facts the entity owns or takes part in, oldest first. Choose **View all in Facts** to open **Facts** filtered to this entity.

An email address can belong to only one entity in a matter.

## Current limitations

- Relationships between entities over time (for example who reported to whom) are not tracked yet.
