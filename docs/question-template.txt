= Extension to Question Template

The template used when a question is being extended.  This
template is responsible for displaying the new document chunk
summaries to the LLM, and asking it to respond to the question
the user wishes to extend (which may be the original topic, or
may be an earlier followup question).

This template is only used when the extension question is the
active (last) question in the conversation.  When the extension
request is in the conversation history, it is rendered as
a regular followup question.

== Variables:

- question : str
    The question that the user wishes to extend the response to
    over the new summaries.  This may be a previous followup
    question, or it may be the original topic of the investigation.

- topic: str
    The original topic of the investigation.

- inputs: List[DocChunkInvestigationSummary]
    The summaries of the new document chunks to be presented to
    the LLM.  (Passed on to `_chunk_smys.j2`.)

== Sub-Templates:

=== `_chunk_smys.j2`

Responsible for rendering of the document chunk summaries.

Unless otherwise instructed in the topic below, act as senior legal and 
investigative professional.

========

The conversation to date has been based on summaries of the top-ranking 
documents returned to the following topic:

<topic>
    {{topic}}
</topic>

Previously, you answered the following request based upon the higher-ranking
summaries:

<request>
     {{question}}
</request>

Above are summaries of additional, lower-ranking documents returned for
the same topic.  Please answer the same request based solely on these new
summaries.

While answering this request , please include inline citations to documents
that support each part of your answer, in the form ``Document XXX''.

Some of the summaries may be for sections of documents.  For these summaries,
please cite the document and section as ``Document XXX§NN'', where NN is
the section number.

Your citations must include the associated paragraph references (¶) whenever they are provided 
in the source material. These paragraph-level citations are essential for 
precise source tracking and must be preserved in the format "Document XXX¶N" 
or "Document XXX§NN¶N" as applicable. 

Present ALL information from these new summaries that relates to the request.
Every statement must be cited using these new summaries only.
Structure your response based on what these summaries contain:

1. "From these new summaries, the following information reinforces previous findings:" 
    [present with new citations] -- include if such information exists

2. "These new summaries provide additional insights:"
    [present with new citations] -- include if such information exists

ALL statements require citations from these new summaries, regardless of whether 
they reinforce previous findings. Do not reference previous documents.
